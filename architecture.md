# Learning Architecture

*Phase 2 output from Blueprint*

## Learning Outcomes

[INFERRED FROM: pantry/source-schedule.md activities and evidence requirements; proposed assessable formulations for author review]

1. **Analyze:** Separate human design decisions from delegated implementation and produce a critique, self-audit, and tool-access record.
2. **Create / Evaluate:** Frame an evidence-supported opportunity and distinguish stakeholder observations from Claude-generated hypotheses.
3. **Create / Evaluate:** Compare two information architectures and justify a journey that makes identity, purpose, and evidence legible.
4. **Create / Evaluate:** Create alternative interaction concepts and defend a choice using accessibility criteria and critically reviewed AI feedback.
5. **Create / Evaluate:** Specify a reusable Figma system with explicit rules for what an implementing agent may change.
6. **Create / Evaluate:** Conduct a bounded Figma-MCP-to-Claude implementation and evaluate screenshot versus structured-context fidelity.
7. **Create / Evaluate:** Design and conduct a workflow with explicit state, context, permissions, failure paths, and human handoffs.
8. **Evaluate:** Defend a working critical journey using design alternatives, implementation history, and accepted or rejected agent proposals.
9. **Create / Evaluate:** Design and test uncertainty, consent, escalation, correction, and undo behaviors with bounded human intervention.
10. **Create / Evaluate:** Compare human usability observations with AI-assisted test findings and prioritize justified design changes.
11. **Create / Evaluate:** Set operating constraints and conduct revisions supported by reliability, latency, cost, privacy, and security evidence.
12. **Create / Evaluate:** Deliver a reproducible experience and portfolio narrative that makes the student's decisions and AI contributions inspectable.
13. **Evaluate:** Defend the experience in a first review round, evaluate peers' evidence, and document unresolved trade-offs.
14. **Create / Evaluate:** Synthesize framing, design, conducting, evaluation, and revision into a final experience and portfolio case study.

## Outcome Map

Chapters 1–2 establish purpose and evidence; 3–5 establish experience structure and design specifications; 6–9 establish implementation direction, architecture, defense, and intervention; 10–12 establish evaluation, operational revision, and delivery; 13–14 integrate the full practice through review and synthesis.

## Sequencing Model and Justification

[INFERRED FROM: the supplied sequence]

Use a cumulative project sequence. Each chapter produces evidence the next chapter needs. A reader may revisit an earlier design when testing changes an assumption. Course weeks remain fixed, but iterative work is explicit.

## Three-Act Learning Arc

[INFERRED FROM: the supplied sequence]

- Establish, Chapters 1–5: frame the problem and design an inspectable experience.
- Build and direct, Chapters 6–9: conduct implementation and design human intervention.
- Evaluate and apply, Chapters 10–14: test, revise, deliver, and defend.

## Prerequisite Dependency Map

1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8. Chapters 7–8 supply a working journey for 9–11. Chapters 10–11 supply evaluation evidence for 12. Chapters 12–14 produce delivery, review, and final synthesis. These are learning dependencies, not a ban on iteration.

## Front-Loading Decisions

Introduce human responsibility, evidence quality, privacy, and accessibility before implementation. Establish Figma and institutional Claude access in Chapter 1; verify the actual MCP route before Chapter 6. Keep detailed platform setup in dated, replaceable sections.

## Assessment Plan

The six submissions are needed: A1 Chapter 2; A2 Chapter 4; A3 Chapter 8; A4 Chapter 9; A5 Chapter 11; A6 Chapters 13–14. Other chapter artifacts feed those submissions. Both presentation groups receive equal requirements and the same revision deadline.
