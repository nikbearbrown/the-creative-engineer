## Proposed hands-on adaptation

Build assistant components for resource cards, source links, buttons, and uncertainty states. Define semantic variables, variants, Auto Layout, long-text behavior, and frame naming. Author a preserve/infer/ask table. Read the chosen component through MCP and inspect whether its rule survived retrieval. Use a deliberate second-primary-button violation as a teaching fixture.

### Candidate conducting prompt

> Inspect the selected component and its retrieved rules. List the constraints you actually received. Preserve tokens and variants; ask before inventing a missing state. Propose a change plan only. Do not assume an annotation was retrieved because it exists visually.
