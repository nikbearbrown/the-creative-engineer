## How to use the films in chapters

Before a film, have learners predict one result and name its possible falsifier. Afterward, require the source record, the actual observation, the limitation, and a next test. Then adapt the experiment to the learner's own product, AI tool, portfolio, or service experience. The learner designs the question and acceptance criteria; Claude performs bounded implementation; the learner inspects and interprets.

Do not import every optional Figma product into the required stack. Figma Design and FigJam through MCP, conducted from NEU Claude, remain primary. Weave is a useful boundary case, not a new subscription requirement. The proposed Claude for Educational AI playlist is a separate future resource, not this existing playlist.
