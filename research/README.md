## Teaching direction

The main manuscript will be written directly in the requested Pragmatist voice: actionable result, conditions, compact worked example, concrete checks, and graduated practice. This is a design-and-conducting course, not fourteen implementation assignments. Students may design their own product or AI tool, portfolio, educational experience, public-interest service, workplace tool, or creative experience.

Figma Design and FigJam are the primary design environment. Claude through the Northeastern account is the demonstrated conducting and implementation environment, as Bear confirms. Students record their actual setup and capabilities. No blanket availability disclaimer should eclipse that demonstrated workflow.

The [Figma for Educational AI playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLW3r2g0eZ8lA) is the empirical teaching spine: what works, what fails, under which conditions, and what remains to test. [Playlist evidence](playlist-evidence.md) maps inspected production records to chapters. YouTube retrieval was throttled; the films themselves were not watched in this pass.
