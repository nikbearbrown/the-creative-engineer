## Reading and project choices

Read in order for the cumulative project, or start with Chapter 6 for the Claude/Figma MCP lab. Use the [Figma for Educational AI playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLW3r2g0eZ8lA) as experimental evidence: inspect what worked, what failed, and what each result establishes. The book uses the NEU Claude workflow confirmed by the author.

Students can design their own product or AI tool, portfolio, educational experience, public-interest service, workplace tool, or creative experience. Design and conducting remain central in every domain.
