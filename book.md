# The Creative Engineer

## Concept

A project-centered book teaching engineers to design meaningful digital experiences and conduct agentic AI through implementation, evaluation, and revision. The reader takes one original product, AI tool, portfolio, educational experience, service, workplace application, or creative experience from problem framing to a defensible delivery.

## Central argument

As agents take on more implementation tasks, engineers must develop explicit design judgment and the ability to frame, direct, inspect, evaluate, and revise agentic work.

This is a teaching thesis, not an empirical claim that AI eliminates all implementation work. Human judgment and AI-generated proposals both require scrutiny.

## Reader and deployment

Graduate engineering students in Digital Experience Engineering, with working proficiency in one programming language and basic software, data, or information-systems familiarity. Prior Figma experience is not required. The book accompanies the fourteen-week online studio and should remain usable outside the course.

## Scope and tools

Figma for Education, Figma Design, and FigJam are the primary design environment. Figma MCP calls run through the intended NEU Claude workflow; Claude conducts implementation under student direction. Institutional client access and connector configuration must be verified during research. Claude Code is the intended repository-based implementation client where institutionally supported.

Personal branding and professional portfolios are explicit applications. Designing the student's own product or AI tool is equally central. All projects produce a portfolio case study showing consequential decisions and evidence.
